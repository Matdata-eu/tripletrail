# Code of conduct

TripleTrail is built by people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience.
We want everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the
project is present, to be able to do so without harassment or hostility.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep feedback specific and about the work.
- Welcome newcomers, and help them find their way.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Unwelcome sexual attention, or sexualised language or images.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging others to do any of the above.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code to **info@matdata.eu**. Reports are read by the
maintainers at Matdata and kept confidential. Security vulnerabilities go through the private
channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

The maintainers look at every report and decide on a response that fits it: a private word, a
public warning, removing content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces.
Someone who is asked to stop is expected to stop at once. Maintainers who break this code are
held to it like anyone else.
